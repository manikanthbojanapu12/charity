✦ Make your first event easy

A little preparation goes a long way.

Tips for first-time event attendees and volunteers.

01

Before you arrive

Read the time and location, check activity requirements and choose comfortable clothing. For outdoor activities, bring water and appropriate sun protection.

02

At the event

Check in with the coordinator and listen to the briefing. Tell the team if you need an adjustment or a different task.

03

After the event

Share feedback, return borrowed equipment and consider a manageable next commitment. Reliable follow-through helps teams plan.

✦ For teams and first-time volunteers

There is a place
for your contribution.

Some roles involve hands-on tasks. Others need planning, welcoming participants or coordinating supplies. You do not need every skill before getting involved.

Choose a role matching your comfort level and availability. Specialist activities require suitable training and guidance from qualified organisers.

✦ Event questions

Know what to expect.

Common questions about event registrations and participation.

Are these real bookable events?

These dates, venues and registrations are demonstration content. Demo registration does not reserve a real event place.

Can I attend with a group?

The preview form supports up to ten participants. For real arrangements, organisers would confirm capacity, roles and accessibility needs.

What if I need an accessible activity?

Contact organisers before a real event to discuss mobility, sensory or communication needs and a role that can be adapted.

Do I need experience?

General roles often begin with orientation. Medical, safeguarding and technical tasks require appropriately trained people.
