✦ Ethical Policies

Zero Hidden FeesCommitment to ethical giving standards.

1. No real payment is collected

The contribution form previews a campaign, frequency and amount. No payment processor is connected and no money changes hands.

2. Monthly giving is a preview

Selecting monthly does not create a recurring charge, subscription or future payment obligation.

3. Receipts and reports are samples

Donation history and downloads use demonstration data. They are not tax receipts, financial statements or evidence of a payment.

4. No refund transaction is available

This website does not collect funds and cannot issue payment refunds. A real service would require an approved cancellation and refund process.

5. Use sample information

Do not enter payment-card details, identity documents or other sensitive information. This demo does not request them.

6. Before a real launch

The organisation should review accurate identity information, approved giving terms, payment handling, privacy practices and donation reporting for the actual service.
